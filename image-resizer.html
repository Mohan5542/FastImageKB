Government & Exam Portal Upload Guidelines

Standard official specifications required by major examination boards and government application systems:

SSC & UPSC Forms 20-50 KB

Photo: 20 KB to 50 KB (JPEG)

Signature: 10 KB to 20 KB (JPEG)

Required dimensions: ~140×60px for signature, ~3.5×4.5cm for photo. Use white background.

Banking & IBPS / SBI 10-50 KB

Photo: 20 KB to 50 KB (200×230 px)

Thumb / Sign: 10 KB to 20 KB (140×60 px)

Signatures must be in black ink on crisp white paper. Check auto-downscale for strict KB limits.

Passport & Visa Portals 100-200 KB

Photo: Max 100 KB to 200 KB

Dimensions: 35mm × 45mm (Square / 600×600 px)

Clean, plain white or off-white background with both ears clearly visible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about target file size compression and photo optimization.

How does the Target KB compression work?

The tool tests compression quality parameters in milliseconds using HTML5 Canvas to produce the highest possible visual clarity that strictly stays below your selected KB limit.

What if a 4K image cannot fit in 20KB even at low quality?

With the "Auto-downscale" option enabled, the engine automatically scales down the pixel dimensions incrementally until the file size comfortably meets your target KB threshold.

Which format should I choose for government forms?

JPG / JPEG is the mandatory universal standard for SSC, UPSC, IBPS, and state PSC job forms. Use WebP for websites and apps, and PNG when you need transparent backgrounds.

Are my photos uploaded or stored anywhere?

Never. All processing happens entirely inside your browser memory. You can even disconnect your Wi-Fi or turn on Airplane mode and the tool will continue working perfectly.

How The Image Resizer Works

1. Binary Search Quality Optimization

Instead of guessing random quality percentages, the tool runs a high-precision binary search algorithm on the HTML5 canvas compression parameter to reach the exact maximum visual fidelity that satisfies your target file size.

2. Intelligent Auto-Downscaling

If an original photo has massive dimensions (e.g. 4000×3000) and cannot fit under 20KB or 50KB through compression alone, the engine automatically steps down pixel dimensions while preserving the exact aspect ratio.

3. Signature Optimization & White Background

Signatures with transparent backgrounds often display black or dark rectangles when converted to JPEG. The "Fill transparent areas with solid white" option automatically creates clean white backgrounds required by exam boards.
