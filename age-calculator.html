Understanding Chronological Age & Exam Cut-off Dates

How age calculations work for UPSC, SSC, banking exams, passport renewals, and insurance:

How is age calculated as of a specific cut-off date?

Most exam notifications (e.g., UPSC CSE, SSC CGL, IBPS PO) require your age as of a specific eligibility date (such as August 1st or January 1st). Simply set the "Age as of Date" field above to the cut-off date mentioned in the notification to verify if you meet the minimum (e.g., 21 years) and maximum (e.g., 32 years) age limits.

Does this calculator account for leap years?

Yes. The algorithm computes exact days per month according to the Gregorian calendar, automatically handling February 29th during leap years.

Is my date of birth stored or sent anywhere?

Never. Brandique Tools computes everything right inside your browser. No personal data or birth dates are ever uploaded, collected, or transmitted.
